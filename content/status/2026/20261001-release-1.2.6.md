## 1.2.6 Quiets Down the Logs

[Lottie4J 1.2.6](https://github.com/lottie4j/lottie4j/releases/tag/v1.2.6) is a small but welcome release: animation lifecycle messages (start, stop, offscreen blend-mode rendering) are now logged at `debug` level instead of `info`.

If an application embeds many `LottiePlayer` instances, or plays animations repeatedly, those `info`-level messages could flood the log output and drown out more important entries. Thanks to [@zapek](https://github.com/zapek) for [the fix](https://github.com/lottie4j/lottie4j/pull/11).

See the [full changelog](/releases/#2026-10-01-126) for the details, or the [GitHub diff](https://github.com/lottie4j/lottie4j/compare/v1.2.5...v1.2.6) between 1.2.5 and 1.2.6.
